🎯 What is GazBoard?

GazBoard is a free, offline-first whiteboard application that puts a blank canvas right on your screen. It's designed for anyone who needs to brainstorm, teach, draw diagrams, crank out quick notes, or mark up existing documents — all without creating an account, signing in, or sending data anywhere.

Unlike web-based whiteboards that lock you out when Wi-Fi drops or services sunset, GazBoard works entirely on your machine. Your drawings, your notes, your documents — they never leave your computer. No telemetry, no cloud dependency, no account. Just open it and start creating, instantly.

✨ Key Features That Make GazBoard Shine

✏️ Versatile Drawing Tools

Use a highly responsive pen or highlighter to sketch ideas, annotate screenshots, or draw flowcharts. Pressure-sensitive for natural strokes.

📝 Sticky Notes Anytime

Plop vibrant sticky notes anywhere on the board. Great for quick reminders, brainstorming sessions, or organizing thoughts visually.

🔷 Easy Shapes for Diagrams

Draw clean rectangles, circles, arrows, and lines using the shape library. Perfect for wireframes, mind maps, or technical diagrams.

📄 Import & Annotate PDF, Word, PowerPoint

Bring in existing files from your computer (PDF, DOCX, PPTX) and write directly on top of them. Highlight key points, fill forms, or annotate presentation drafts.

🔒 100% Offline & Private

No account. No cloud. No telemetry. Everything stays local on your machine. You own your data, period.

🖥️ Native Windows & Linux App

Built with Electron, GazBoard runs smoothly on both Windows and Linux. Open a clean, focused window and get to work instantly.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is GazBoard really free?

Yes! Complete free. No hidden costs, no "pro" tier, no feature locks. Download, install, and use as much as you like.

Will my files or board data be uploaded?

Absolutely not. Every stroke, note, shape, and imported document stays local on your disk. GazBoard has no network access for data collection or sync. What you draw is yours alone.

Can I use it without an internet connection?

Yes. That's the whole point. Download the installer once, then you can run GazBoard completely offline — even on a long flight or in a basement with no signal.

Does it work with tablet pens / stylus?

Yes! GazBoard supports pressure-sensitive stylus input on compatible Windows devices (like Surface pens), making it great for natural handwriting and drawing.

Can I import my lesson slides?

Absolutely. GazBoard lets you import PDF, Word (.docx), and PowerPoint (.pptx) files directly. Then you can underline, circle, or scribble side notes right on top.
